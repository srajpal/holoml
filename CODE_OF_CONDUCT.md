# Code of conduct

HoloML is a small project, kept in spare time. Everyone who takes part
in it, in issues, pull requests, reviews, and anywhere else the project
is talked about, is asked to keep to this.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people; assume the other
  person means well, and say what you mean plainly and kindly.
- No harassment: no insults, no threats, no unwelcome personal or sexual
  attention, no remarks about who someone is (their sex, gender, race,
  religion, age, disability, or where they come from), and no publishing
  of anyone's private details.
- Keep to the subject: the language, its tools, its guides, and its
  examples.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here breaks this, tell the owner privately: on
the repository's page, open **Security**, then **Report a
vulnerability** (GitHub's private reporting, which only the owner
reads), and say that it is about conduct. Do not open a public issue
for it. Anything posted here can also be reported to GitHub with the
"Report content" entry in its menu.

The owner reads every report, keeps the reporter's name private, and
may remove a comment, close a thread, or block an account from the
repository. This is a personal project; please allow a few days for an
answer.

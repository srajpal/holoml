Ocean tunnel

An aquarium to walk through: a glass tunnel under a big tank, where sharks, a sea turtle, tuna, and schools of fish swim over and around you, among rocks, plants, and rising bubbles, with light from the surface playing over the sand. Feed them, and click a fish to read about it. Written in HoloML 0.2, an open markup language for 3D websites.

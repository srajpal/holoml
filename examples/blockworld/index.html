Blockworld

A very small block game: break and place blocks, find five gems in the stone, and see day turn to night. Written in HoloML, an open markup language for 3D websites (version 0.2, a draft).

This browser cannot show HoloML pages. Open index.holoml in a browser that can, such as HyperSpace 3D.

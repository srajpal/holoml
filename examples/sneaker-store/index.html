Sneaker store

A sneaker store to walk through: one shoe in ten colourways on its walls, each loaded as you come near. Open one to turn it over, choose its colour and size, and add it to your cart. Written in HoloML 0.2, an open markup language for 3D websites.

This browser cannot show HoloML pages. Open index.holoml in a browser that can, such as HyperSpace 3D.

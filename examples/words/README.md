# Words in a room

A short HoloML 0.3 page: three walls of welcome signs, in English,
Arabic, and Hebrew. It shows `lang` and `dir` (HoloML 0.3): the page
says its language once, on the root; each wall's group says its own;
Arabic and Hebrew run from right to left, each panel's lines starting
at its right edge; and a screen reader reads each sign in its
language's voice. The screen's text uses `dir="auto"`, which follows its
first letter.

Open `index.holoml` in a browser that shows HoloML, such as HyperSpace
3D. Everything here is text; there are no models, pictures, or sounds.

The page is the HoloML Authors', under Apache 2.0, as the repository's
code is.

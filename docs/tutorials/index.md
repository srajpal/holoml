# Tutorials

Lessons to follow from the start, in order. Each builds something that
works, one step at a time, and ends with a page you can open in
HyperSpace 3D.

1. [Your first HoloML page](first-page.md): a model to walk around, with
   light, a label, and a link to a second page.
2. [Your first script](first-script.md): a lamp that a click switches on
   and off, a sound, and text on the screen that follows along.

When you have finished them, the [how-to guides](../how-to/index.md)
show how to do one thing at a time, and the
[specification](../../SPEC.md) says everything a page can hold.
